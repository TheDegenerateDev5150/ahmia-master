{% trans "1. What Does Ahmia Do?" %}

{% blocktrans %} Ahmia is a non-commercial, privacy-focused search engine that indexes and displays links to publicly accessible `.onion` services on the Tor network. It provides keyword-based search functionality only. It does not host, upload, or distribute `.onion` content. Ahmia does not offer user accounts, personalization, or any form of interactive services. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %} Ahmia complies with online safety regulations and laws, including the UK Online Safety Act and the Australian Online Safety Act. Users can report harmful or illegal content. Ahmia actively filters search results and may reject or intercept search terms that match its safety filters. When a search matches a CSAM-related or other safety filter, Ahmia may show self-help resources, research surveys, or an alternative result view instead of ordinary search results. Matched search terms may be assigned to an intervention category so that a category-specific message or resource can be shown. The category describes the matched query and is not used to create a persistent user profile. The types of content that Ahmia filters may grow in the future as safety rules change and technology for finding harmful content gets better, all while keeping Ahmia's promise to protect user privacy and anonymity. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %} Ahmia may conduct user interface experiments, such as A/B tests, to evaluate how effectively help links, messages, or alternative search result views reach users who submit filtered or high-risk search terms. These tests may involve different layouts, category-specific messages, help resources, or redirect options, and may use randomized experiment labels. Ahmia may measure aggregate interactions such as help-link clicks to evaluate these interventions. No accounts, persistent user identifiers, or user profiles are created for these experiments. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %} Digital Services Act (DSA) Statement: Ahmia is a small-scale, non-commercial academic project that processes approximately 1,715,358 searches per month, with the total number of unique users being significantly lower. It is designed with privacy-by-design principles and does not use cookies, store IP addresses, or create user accounts or persistent user identifiers; thus, Ahmia cannot estimate the number of users beyond the search count. Most users access the service via the Tor Browser, making it technically impossible to determine their geographic origin or the number of EU-based users. No IP address data is available for the `.onion` version of Ahmia. {% endblocktrans %}
